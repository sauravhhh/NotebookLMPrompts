The source — exactly as GP Singh said it

“Follow this, It would help. Revise these basic materials and cover them if you have not done so already”

Basic materials

  • Polity — Laxmikanth
  • Modern History — Spectrum
  • Culture — Nitin Singhania + Class 11 Fine Arts book
  • Geography — New NCERTs 6–12
  • General Science — NCERTs 6–10
  • Environment — Various chapters in New Geography NCERTs and Science NCERTs
  • Economy — NCERT Class 11 and 12 Macroeconomics only + Current Affairs / Newspaper

Advanced materials

“If you have time and you have covered / revised the above, you can do advanced material as follows”

  • Polity — Indian Year Book Chapter 1, 2, 3
  • History — Old NCERTs Class 11 and 12 and Tamil Nadu Board
  • Culture — CCRT website
  • Geography — GC Leong
  • General Science — Selected topics from Class 11 and 12 books like Gene Editing and Evolution
  • Environment — ShankarIAS + Schedule and Index of major environmental acts
  • Economy — SriramIAS + Economic Survey
  • Map work — General Idea of World and Indian Map, Protected areas and associated rivers, Ramsar sites, selective reading from Majid Hussain's book on Maps
